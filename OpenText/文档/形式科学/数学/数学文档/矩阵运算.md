##### 矩阵运算
- 矩阵运算
	- **矩阵运算**是对[[矩阵]]及其元素进行的一系列[[运算]]和[[运算律]], 矩阵运算通常是对[[线性变换]]运算的形式化和具体化, 例如矩阵加减乘积可表示线性变换的加减复合, 还有一些矩阵运算用于衡量矩阵的性能表现
		- [[累加运算]]
			- $S = \sum_{i=1}^{m} \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}$
		- [[连乘运算]]
			- $P = \prod_{i=1}^{m} \prod_{j=1}^{n} a_{ij}$
		- [[矩阵加法]]
			- $(A \pm B)_{ij} = a_{ij} \pm b_{ij}$
		- [[矩阵数乘]]
			- $(kA)_{ij} = k \cdot a_{ij}$
		- [[矩阵乘法]]
			- $(AB)_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^p a_{ik} b_{kj}$
		- [[矩阵的幂]]
			- $A^k = A \cdot A \cdot \ldots \cdot A$
		- [[矩阵转置]]
			- $(A^T)_{ij} = a_{ji}$
		- [[共轭转置]]
			- $(A^*)_{ij} = \overline{a_{ji}}$
		- [[可逆矩阵|矩阵的逆]]
			- $A^{-1} \iff AA^{-1} = A^{-1}A = I$
		- [[克罗内克积]]
			- $A \otimes B = \begin{bmatrix} a_{11}B & \cdots & a_{1n}B \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1}B & \cdots & a_{mn}B \end{bmatrix}$
		- [[特征值和特征向量|矩阵的谱]]
			- $\sigma(A) = \{ \lambda \mid \det(\lambda I - A) = 0 \}$
		- [[矩阵的迹]]
			- $\text{tr}(A) = \sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}$
		- [[行列式]]
			- $\det(A) = |A| = \sum_{\sigma \in S_n} \text{sgn}(\sigma) \prod_{i=1}^n a_{i, \sigma(i)}$
		- [[二次型]]
			- $f(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x}^T A \mathbf{x} = \sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij} x_i x_j$
		- [[奇异值]]
			- $\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i(A^T A)}$
		- [[谱半径]]
			- $\rho(A) = \max \{|\lambda_1|, |\lambda_2|, \dots, |\lambda_n|\}$
		- [[矩阵范数]]
			- $\|A\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i,j} |a_{ij}|^2}$
			- $\|A\|_p = \sup_{x \neq 0} \frac{\|Ax\|_p}{\|x\|_p}$
		- [[矩阵指数]]
			- $e^A = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{A^k}{k!}$
