##### 负二项分布
- 负二项分布
	- **负二项分布**是[[离散随机变量]]的[[概率分布]]
		- $X \sim \mathrm{NegBin}(r,p)$
		- $\displaystyle P(X=k) = \binom{k+r-1}{r-1} p^r (1-p)^k$, $k=0,1,2,\dots$
		- $\mathrm {E}(X)=r\frac{1-p}{p}$
		- $\mathrm{Var}(X) = r\frac{1-p}{p^2}$
