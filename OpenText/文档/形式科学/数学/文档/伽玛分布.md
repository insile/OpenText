##### 伽玛分布
- 伽玛分布
	- **伽玛分布**是[[连续随机变量]]的[[概率分布]]
		- $X \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(\alpha, \beta)$
		- $\displaystyle f(x; \alpha, \beta) = \frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha} x^{\alpha-1} e^{-x/\beta}$ $x > 0$
		- $\mathrm{E}[X] = \alpha\beta$
		- $\mathrm{Var}(X) = \alpha\beta^2$
