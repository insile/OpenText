##### 酉矩阵
- 酉矩阵
	- **酉矩阵**是指[[共轭转置]]等于[[可逆矩阵|逆矩阵]]的[[复数]][[矩阵]]. 设 $U \in \mathbb{C}^{n \times n}$ 为复方阵, 若 $U^{-1}=U^H$, 即 $U^H U = U U^H = I$, 则称 $U$ 为酉矩阵. 酉矩阵是[[正交矩阵|实正交矩阵]]在复数域的完整推广. 酉矩阵列向量两两[[正交]]行向量两两正交, 其行列向量组都是[[正交组|标准正交组]]. [[酉算子]]在任意标准正交基下表示为酉矩阵
		- $U^H=U^{-1}\iff U^HU=UU^H=I$ 
		- $U^HU = \begin{bmatrix} \langle \mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_1 \rangle & \langle \mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_2 \rangle & \cdots & \langle \mathbf{u}_1, \mathbf{u}_n \rangle \\ \langle \mathbf{u}_2, \mathbf{u}_1 \rangle & \langle \mathbf{u}_2, \mathbf{u}_2 \rangle & \cdots & \langle \mathbf{u}_2, \mathbf{u}_n \rangle \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \langle \mathbf{u}_n, \mathbf{u}_1 \rangle & \langle \mathbf{u}_n, \mathbf{u}_2 \rangle & \cdots & \langle \mathbf{u}_n, \mathbf{u}_n \rangle \end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
