##### 矩阵转置
- 矩阵转置
	- **矩阵转置**是指将[[矩阵]]的行与列位置互换得到新矩阵的[[运算]]. 设 $A = (a_{ij})_{m \times n} \in \mathbb{F}^{m \times n}$ 为矩阵, 则其转置矩阵定义为 $A^T = (a_{ji})_{n \times m}$
		- $(A^T)_{ij}=a_{ji}$


>[!example]- 矩阵转置
> - $(A^T)^T=A$
> - $(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T$
> - $(AB)^T=B^TA^T$
> - $(kA)^T=kA^T$
> - $(A^k)^T = (A^T)^k$
> - $(A^{-1})^T = (A^T)^{-1}$
> - $\operatorname{tr}(A^T) = \operatorname{tr}(A)$
> - $\det(A^T) = \det(A)$
