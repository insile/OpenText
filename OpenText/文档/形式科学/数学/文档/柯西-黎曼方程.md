##### 柯西-黎曼方程
- 柯西-黎曼方程
	- **柯西-黎曼方程**是描述[[复函数]]成为[[解析函数]]或[[全纯函数]]的必要条件, 由两个[[偏微分方程]]组成. 设复函数 $f(z)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$, $z=x+iy$, 则复导数的存在要求极限与自变量的趋近路径无关, 考虑实轴 $\Delta z = \Delta x$ 和虚轴 $\Delta z = i\Delta y$ 两种特殊路径, 它们必然相等, 分离实部与虚部可得到柯西–黎曼方程. 柯西-黎曼方程仅是函数全纯的必要条件, 若加上偏导数连续性, 则成为充要条件. 若 $u$, $v$ 在该点邻域内一阶偏导数存在且连续, 并满足柯西–黎曼方程, 则 $f$ 在该点可复导
		- $\displaystyle f'(z) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(z + \Delta x) - f(z)}{\Delta x} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + i \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}$
		- $\displaystyle f'(z) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(z + i\Delta y) - f(z)}{i\Delta y} = \frac{1}{i} \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} - i \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$
		- $\displaystyle \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = -i \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}$  $\begin{cases} \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \dfrac{\partial v}{\partial y} \\[2mm] \dfrac{\partial u}{\partial y} = -\,\dfrac{\partial v}{\partial x} \end{cases}$
