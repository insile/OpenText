##### t-分布
- t-分布
	- **t-分布**是[[连续随机变量]]的[[概率分布]]
		- $X \sim t(n)$
		- $\displaystyle f(x; n) = \frac{\Gamma(\frac{n+1}{2})}{\sqrt{n\pi}\Gamma(\frac{n}{2})} \left(1 + \frac{x^2}{n}\right)^{-\frac{n+1}{2}}$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$
		- $\mathrm{E}[X] = 0$
		- $\mathrm{Var}(X) = \frac{n}{n-2}$
