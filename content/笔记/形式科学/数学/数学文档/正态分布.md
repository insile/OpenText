##### 正态分布
- 正态分布
	- **正态分布**是[[连续随机变量]]的[[概率分布]], 其概率密度函数为归一化的[[高斯函数]]
		- $X \sim \mathrm{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$
		- $\displaystyle f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma} \exp\Big(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\Big)$, $x\in\mathbb{R}$
		- $\mathrm {E}(X)=\mu$
		- $\mathrm{Var}(X) = \sigma^2$
		- $\mathbf{X} \sim \mathrm{N}_n(\boldsymbol{\mu}, \Sigma)$
		- $\displaystyle f(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n/2} |\Sigma|^{1/2}} \exp\left( -\frac{1}{2} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu})^\top \Sigma^{-1} (\mathbf{x} - \boldsymbol{\mu}) \right)$, $\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^n$
		- $\mathrm {E}(\mathbf{X})=\boldsymbol{\mu}$
		- $\mathrm{Var}(\mathbf{X}) = \Sigma$
