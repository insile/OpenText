##### 矩阵的迹
- 矩阵的迹
	- **矩阵的迹** ${\rm tr}(A)$ 是[[方阵]]主对角线上各个元素的总和. [[算子的迹]] ${\rm tr}(T)$ 等于线性算子关于任意基的矩阵的迹 ${\rm tr}(A)$, 等于所有[[特征值和特征向量|特征值]]之和
		- $\displaystyle{\rm tr}(A)=\sum^{n}_{i=1}a_{ii}$
		- $\displaystyle{\rm tr}(A)=\sum^{n}_{i=1}\lambda_{i}$


>[!example]- 矩阵的迹
> - $\operatorname{tr}(A + B) = \operatorname{tr}(A) + \operatorname{tr}(B)$
> - $\operatorname{tr}(kA) = k \operatorname{tr}(A)$
> - $\operatorname{tr}(AB) = \operatorname{tr}(BA)$
> - $\operatorname{tr}(A^T) = \operatorname{tr}(A)$
> - $\operatorname{tr}(A^*) = \overline{\operatorname{tr}(A)}$
