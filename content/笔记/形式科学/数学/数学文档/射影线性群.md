##### 射影线性群
- 射影线性群
	- **射影线性群**是由[[射影变换|可逆射影变换]]及其[[复合映射]]构成的[[群]], 并且是[[李群]]
		- $\mathrm{PGL}(n+1, \mathbb{F}) = \mathrm{GL}(n+1, \mathbb{F}) \Big/ Z\bigl(\mathrm{GL}(n+1, \mathbb{F})\bigr)$, $Z\bigl(\mathrm{GL}(n+1, \mathbb{F})\bigr) = \{\lambda I_{n+1} \mid \lambda \in \mathbb{F}^\times\}$
